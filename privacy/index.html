Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 23, 2026

What We Collect

Boards stores the links you save, including URLs, titles, and any categories or notes you add. When you use the iOS app, your authentication session is stored locally on your device.

How We Use It

Your data is used solely to provide the Boards service — saving, organizing, and displaying your links. We use AI (Claude by Anthropic) to suggest categories and enrich link metadata. Link URLs and titles are sent to AI services for this purpose only.

Data Storage

Your data is stored in Supabase (hosted on AWS). Authentication is handled by Supabase Auth using magic link email login. We do not store passwords.

Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

Supabase — database and authentication. Anthropic (Claude) — AI categorization. OpenAI — fallback AI processing. These services process data as needed to provide functionality.

What We Don't Do

We do not sell your data. We do not run ads. We do not use tracking or analytics. We do not share your data with anyone except the services listed above, which are used solely to operate the product.

Your Data

You can export or delete your data at any time by contacting us. Your links are yours.
