# Cross-Category Connections

> **Status:** ⚠️ Partial
> **Brand Principle:** One place, whole life
> **Key Personas:** Sound & Scene Curator, Multidisciplinary Maker, Researcher, Cultural Omnivore, Design Technologist

Don't silo interests. A person's music taste, design references, and shopping finds are all facets of who they are. Let connections emerge.

---

## What's Shipped

### Sub-Tag Filtering
- Groups related items within a category by keyword
- Shows shared themes within a single domain

### Widget Cross-References
- "Complete the Look" and other widgets surface items that complement existing pins
- Widget suggestions draw from across the collection

### Domain Grouping
- Pins from the same domain naturally cluster
- Content type awareness crosses categories

---

## What's Planned

### Related Pins
- "Related to this" links on individual pins
- Algorithm: shared tags, similar domains, temporal proximity, AI semantic similarity
- Surface in pin detail view or as widget

### Cross-Category Discovery
- "Pins in [Category A] that relate to [Category B]" queries
- Theme-based browsing that spans categories
- "You have 5 pins about brutalism across Architecture, Fashion, and Music"

### Connection Visualization
- Graph view showing relationships between pins and categories
- Cluster detection: auto-identify thematic groups
- Timeline overlay: see when connections formed

### Smart Boards
- Auto-populated boards based on detected themes
- "Your week in design" or "Music + events" dynamic collections
- Rules-based: update automatically as new pins are added

## Persona Fit

| Persona | Why Cross-Category Matters |
|---------|---------------------------|
