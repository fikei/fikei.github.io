A community-funded DJ residency.

We're Agape — a 16-person co-op in a massive old Victorian in San Francisco's Mission District. Over the last few years we've become home to DJs, artists, builders, and people making interesting things. We built a proper listening room in our basement with a Neptune sound system because we wanted a place to throw great parties and listen to music together.

Now we want to try an experiment: we'll select a DJ to come live with us. Room and board included. You get the sound system, the decks, time to work on your craft, and a place inside the community. Before you leave, we film a one-hour set in the basement.

The residency is funded entirely by application fees. The goal isn't to make money — it's to see whether enough people believe in the idea to keep this room open as an artist space long-term. Every application funds opportunities for future artists, selected or not.

Residents receive

  • Room and board for the residency window
  • The Neptune sound system and Pioneer decks, downstairs from your room
  • Time to work on your craft inside a creative community
  • A one-hour filmed set in the basement before you leave

What we ask

  • Live with us — this is a home, not a hotel
  • An interview and house approval before selection
  • The set: recorded or performed by the end of your stay
  • A residency application fee, which funds the program
