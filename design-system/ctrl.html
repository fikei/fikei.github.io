Introduction

The global design language for ctrl.rodeo — Boards, Events, Soundscape, Systemic, and Favicon. Minimal, high-contrast, monospace-first. Product systems (like Agape recruiting) override this one for their product.

Principles

The five commitments behind the aesthetic.

  • High contrast — pure black and white as primary colors.
  • Monospace-first — technical, code-like; serif only for display headings.
  • Minimal — borders over fills, function over decoration.
  • Dark-first — dark mode default with an optional light mode (the toggle in the top bar).
  • Accessible — clear visual hierarchy, readable text at small sizes.
