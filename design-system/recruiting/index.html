Getting started / Introduction

Introduction

The design system for ctrl.rodeo/applications — the app that moves a person from application to Agape housemate. One screen of rows carries the whole funnel, so nearly every rule here is about earning the right to put something on a row.

Apply→ Inbox→ Candidates→ Openings→ Screening→ Visit · decision

Notes

Dark-first, Inter/system sans, rounded rectangles for actions and pills for state. Blue stages are automated by the app; grey ones are human-run (for now). Source of truth for behavior: docs/ux/recruiting-row-states.md in the repo; this site is the articulated why.

Principles

Every rule below was earned by deleting something that violated it.

·
One primary action per rowThe right rail answers "whose move is it?" with exactly one button. If a row needs two buttons, the state machine is wrong — demote one to the context tier or an icon.
·
Two tiers: action, then contextPrimary on top; timestamps, secondary links, and who/when live in a quiet caption beneath.
·
Name the goal, not the transport"Ask for coverage," never "Post to Discord" as a goal label. Mechanism belongs in subcopy.
·
Never explain the UINo "tap a time to book it." Outcome copy is welcome: "Invites will be sent to both of you, with an email introduction."
·
Never be redundantOne quote plus one interpretation line. If a badge says it, the subline doesn't repeat it.
·
Select, then confirmAnything that commits takes two taps: a selection state, then a labeled confirm. Never a native dialog in a polished flow.
·
Whitespace over chromeOverflow menus are borderless ⋮ until hover. Hint text and toolbars get removed before a divider gets added.
·
Dates have to line upA candidate appears under a listing only when their window genuinely overlaps it. "Flexible" must be their word, not our guess.

Decision log

Why it is the way it is — dated, so future arguments have receipts.

Jul 22
Collective votes replaced the single decider.

Culture fit is the house's call; the app's job is quorum, blindness until you've voted, and a hard veto.

Jul 22
AI suggestions replaced by deterministic placement.

A candidate is in every listing they qualify for, by rule. Confidence percentages were deleted — dates either line up or they don't.

Jul 22
Discord posts are manual-with-preview, for now.

A human sees the exact message before the house does. Auto-posting is a cutover we'll earn, not a default.

Jul 22
Pick-a-time beat the Discord post as the schedule primary.

Self-serve booking is the fast path; coverage is the fallback, so it reads as one ("Ask for coverage").

Jul 22
The rail became two tiers with a fixed-width column.

One primary, quiet context beneath, every edge aligned. The alternative — badges, timestamps, and two buttons jostling inline — is where the old design died.

Jul 22
Accessible-dark palette softened to friendly mid-tones.

Strict AA darks read as heavy. The system trades measured contrast for warmth at semibold sizes — a deliberate, documented exception.

Jul 27
The outstanding list shipped in one pass.

Phase B (new-application pings + the update-email tray), draft listings from occupancy gaps, awaiting-claim and give-decision states, follow-up staleness, visit drafts, the flexible ±1 month dial, and auto-posting as a settings toggle rather than a deploy.

Jul 22
The profile footer joined the state machine.

The lime/red decision pills were the last pre-v3 survivors; the footer now shows the same primary as the person's row.

Shape & spacing

A pill means state; a rectangle means action.

State · pill Card · 12px Modal · 16px

Notes

  • Radii: 6 (avatars) · 8 (buttons, inputs) · 12 (cards, rows) · 16 (modals) · 999 (pills).
  • Spacing scale: 4 / 8 / 12 / 16 / 20 / 24 / 32 / 40. Lists breathe with row padding, not dividers-on-dividers.
  • Same-size buttons in a column share a fixed width (172px in rails) so edges never drift.

Buttons

Rounded rectangles, white text on state hues, one primary per context.

Notes

  • Grey is the resting default; a hue signals the state advanced.
  • Secondaries are text links or small icon buttons — never a second full button.
  • Hover brightens ~8%; no elevation or shadow play.

Chips & badges

Pills carry state. Tinted background + matching ink, never solid fills.

Notes

Blue tint = scheduled/placed facts. Grey = counts and timestamps. Amber tint = something is owed (an update email, a stale follow-up). Placement pills are one-per-room with the open date — tapping ✕ in the profile removes with a tombstone so the auto-sweep never re-adds.

Automation audit

Every automated message the app sends is visible in one channel, so the house can audit the robot and subgroups can be given access to just that channel.

🤖 New application — Marisa → #recruiting-society

📥 Marisa Maccaro applied — Full-time · Sep 2026

Notes

  • #recruiting-automation is the audit hub (formerly #recruiting-interviews — same channel, renamed). Claim posts live here; everything else is mirrored here.
  • #recruiting-society is the members channel: new-application pings, Intro Call notes and recordings, live-call announcements.
  • Mirrors are one compact line — label, target channel, first line of the real message. Best-effort: an audit failure never breaks the automation it describes, and messages that already went to the automation channel aren't echoed.
  • DMs are audited too — the house sees that a claimer confirmation went out without seeing the thread.
  • New-application pings only cover the last 14 days, so turning them on never dumps the backlog.

Stage machine

The visual system sits on deterministic, server-side rules — never client opinion.

1
Inbox → Candidates3+ votes averaging ≥ 3.5. One veto (note required) auto-archives with an update email owed. Budget under $1,500/mo auto-archives at load.
2
Candidates → OpeningsAuto-placement into every open listing whose dates genuinely line up (track match, budget covers rent, window overlap — no padding). Removals are tombstoned; the sweep never re-adds.
3
Openings → ScreeningAvailability parsed from replies (thread-matched, any sender address); calls booked in-app, via a Discord claim, or picked up automatically from the shared calendar.
4
Recruiter-confirmed dates winA confirmed window replaces parsed text everywhere — display, filters, placement — and is exact: no "flexible" escape hatch.
5
Passing requires outreachEvery rejection queues an update email. "Archived" without one is reserved for history imports.
