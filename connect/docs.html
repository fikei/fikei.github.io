ctrl.rodeo

Connector Documentation

Connect your curated library to Claude. Search your saves, get taste-aware recommendations, and capture new finds — all from within Claude.

Description

ctrl.rodeo is a personal curation platform for collecting and organizing everything that matters — products, articles, music, restaurants, travel, fashion, and more. The connector gives Claude access to your curated library so it can provide recommendations informed by your actual taste.

Features

  • Search your library by keyword, board, content type, or tags
  • Browse boards (Wear, Listen, Read, Home, Use, Eat, Go, Follow, Watch)
  • Get your taste profile — AI-generated patterns across your saves
  • Save new pins back to your library with full enrichment
  • Auto-capture: Claude saves items when you express a clear choice signal
  • Privacy controls: choose exactly what Claude can see

Installation

Add the connector in Claude settings or click the install link:

Server URL: https://yfhudwakpgzswiylhfbh.supabase.co/functions/v1/mcp-server

Claude handles the OAuth flow automatically. You'll be redirected to ctrl.rodeo to sign in and choose your privacy level. No API keys or configuration needed.

Configuration

After connecting, manage your settings in the Boards app:

  • Privacy Level — Choose what Claude can see: Taste Only (tags/categories), Library (titles + tags), or Full Access (everything including URLs)
  • Board Visibility — Toggle which boards Claude can access
  • Auto-Save — Let Claude save pins when you say things like "I'll get that" or "add to cart"

Open the settings at ctrl.rodeo/boards (gear icon).

Tools

search_pins read
Search saved items by keyword, board, content type, or tags. Returns matching pins filtered by privacy settings.
get_board read
Get all items in a board (e.g., Wear, Listen, Read). Supports pagination.
get_boards_list read
List all boards with item counts and descriptions.
get_taste_profile read
Get the user's taste profile — cross-board patterns, top tags, brand affinities, and aesthetic identity.
get_recent_saves read
Get recent saves. Useful for understanding current interests.
save_pin write
Save a URL to the library. Automatically enriched with images, categories, entities, and tags. Idempotent — saving the same URL twice returns the existing pin.
get_connector_context read
Orientation tool — returns board taxonomy, content types, tag system, and composition guidance.

Usage Examples

"I need new running shoes. What brands do I already like?"
Claude calls get_taste_profile to understand your brand affinities, then search_pins with board="wear" and tag="running" to find specific saves. It recommends brands based on your actual collection.
"Find me a restaurant like that Italian place I saved."
Claude calls search_pins in the "eat" board to find your Italian saves, identifies what you liked (taste tags, entities), then uses those patterns to suggest similar restaurants.
"Help me plan a weekend trip to Portland."
Claude searches your "go", "eat", and "use" boards for Portland saves, checks your taste profile for travel patterns, and builds an itinerary informed by places and things you already love.
"That Aesop hand cream looks perfect — save it."
Claude calls save_pin with the product URL. The pin is auto-categorized into the right board, enriched with images and tags, and appears in your library within seconds.
"What's my design style based on my saves?"
Claude calls get_taste_profile and get_board for "home", analyzes cross-board patterns between your home, wear, and follow boards, and describes your aesthetic identity with specific examples from your library.
